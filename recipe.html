Classic Baked Cheesecake

Classic Baked Cheesecake is a rich, creamy, and smooth dessert made with cream cheese, eggs, sugar, and a buttery biscuit crust. It is baked until set and then chilled before serving. It is one of the most popular desserts around the world.

Ingredients

  • 200 g Digestive Biscuits
  • 100 g Melted Butter
  • 500 g Cream Cheese
  • 150 g Sugar
  • 2 Eggs
  • 200 ml Fresh Cream
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
  • 1 tbsp Cornflour

Instructions

  1. Crush the biscuits and mix them with melted butter.
  2. Press the mixture into the base of a cake pan.
  3. Beat the cream cheese and sugar until smooth.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time and mix well.
  5. Mix in the fresh cream, vanilla extract, and cornflour.
  6. Pour the filling over the biscuit base.
  7. Bake at 160°C for about 50–60 minutes.
  8. Allow the cheesecake to cool, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours before serving.
